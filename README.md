# Supplementary materials for LLM4ST MLR

These materials accompany *Large Language Models for Software Traceability: A Multivocal Literature Review*.

The synthesis includes 61 sources, comprising 43 published sources and 18 grey-literature sources. Source IDs S01–S53 and G1–G8 match the manuscript. EX1 and EX2 identify candidates excluded during quality assessment and do not enter the synthesis.

## Contents

- `01_review_need_assessment/` contains search exports and screening records for the 60 prior reviews used to establish the review need. These 60 reviews are separate from the 61 primary sources.
- `02_primary_study_search/` contains database and web/video search records, screening decisions, archived grey-source evidence, and the two reviewers' independent quality and evidence-maturity assessments.
- `03_search_validation/` contains the ten-source search-validation set and database coverage results.
- `04_data_extraction/paper_data_extract_sum.xlsx` contains the combined source index and source-level extraction records for all 61 included sources.
- `04_data_extraction/RQ1/` through `RQ7/` contain the normalized records, coding rules, and synthesis tables for the corresponding research questions. Their analytical units are documented in `04_data_extraction/README.md`.

Search exports retain retrieved records, including records later excluded. Screening records document the decisions at each stage. Independent reviewer scores are preserved separately from the final inclusion and evidence-maturity classifications.

The synthesis covers 113 traceability tasks, 139 distinct target-LLM operations, 281 target-LLM uses, 285 limitation statements, and 258 in-scope future research directions. Two additional future statements about publication or degree completion are retained as out of scope.

Only the current extraction and synthesis files are retained. Scripts, JSON files, caches, working templates, redundant extraction workbooks, and local copies of published papers are not included. Grey-source evidence is archived where needed to locate the extracted content.
